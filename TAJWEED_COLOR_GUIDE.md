# 🎨 Tajweed Color Coding Guide

## Visual Reference for Quranic Tajweed Rules

This guide explains the color-coding system implemented in the Quran Reader to help users learn and apply Tajweed rules correctly.

---

## 📖 What is Tajweed?

**Tajweed** (تجويد) is the set of rules for proper pronunciation during Quranic recitation. The word "Tajweed" means "to make better" or "to improve." These rules ensure that each letter is pronounced correctly with its proper characteristics.

---

## 🌈 Color-Coded Tajweed Rules

### 1. 🟢 **Ghunnah (Nasalization)** - Green
**Color**: `#4CAF50` (Material Green)

**Rule**: A nasal sound produced when reciting certain letters, particularly:
- ن (Noon) with Shadda (ّ) or Sukun (ْ)
- م (Meem) with Shadda (ّ) or Sukun (ْ)
- Two consecutive nasalized letters

**Example Words**: مِنْ, أَنْ, مَّا, نَّاس

**Duration**: 2 beats (counts)

---

### 2. 🟣 **Qalqalah (Echo)** - Purple
**Color**: `#9C27B0` (Material Purple)

**Rule**: A bouncing or echoing sound when these letters have Sukun (ْ):
- ق (Qaf)
- ط (Taa)
- ب (Baa)
- ج (Jeem)
- د (Daal)

**Mnemonic**: "قُطْبُ جَد" (Qutb Jad)

**Example Words**: أَحَد, رَزْق, يَعْبُد

---

### 3. 🔴 **Madd (Elongation)** - Red
**Color**: `#F44336` (Material Red)

**Rule**: Prolonged vowel sounds, including:
- Natural Madd: آ, و, ي
- Connected Madd (Madd Muttasil)
- Separated Madd (Madd Munfasil)
- Necessary Madd (Madd Lazim)

**Duration**: 
- Natural: 2 beats
- Connected: 4-5 beats
- Separated: 2-5 beats
- Necessary: 6 beats

**Example Words**: آمَنَ, يَقُولُ, طَائِفَة

---

### 4. ⚫ **Idghaam (Merging)** - Gray
**Color**: `#757575` (Material Gray)

**Rule**: Merging ن (Noon) Sakinah or Tanween with following letters:
- ي (Ya)
- ر (Ra)
- م (Meem)
- ل (Lam)
- و (Waw)
- ن (Noon)

**Mnemonic**: "يَرْمُلُون" (Yarmaloon)

**Types**:
- With Ghunnah: ينمو
- Without Ghunnah: رل

**Example Words**: مِنْ رَبِّهِمْ, مَنْ يَعْمَلْ

---

### 5. 🟡 **Ikhfa (Hiding)** - Amber/Yellow
**Color**: `#FFC107` (Material Amber)

**Rule**: Hiding the sound of ن (Noon) Sakinah or Tanween before these letters:
ص ذ ث ك ج ش ق س د ط ز ف ت ض ظ

**Total**: 15 letters

**How to Pronounce**: Pronounce with a nasal sound (Ghunnah) for 2 beats while hiding the Noon sound.

**Example Words**: مِنْ شَيْء, مَنْ كَانَ, أَنْصَار

---

### 6. 🟠 **Iqlab (Conversion)** - Orange
**Color**: `#FF9800` (Material Orange)

**Rule**: Converting ن (Noon) Sakinah or Tanween to م (Meem) when followed by ب (Ba).

**How to Pronounce**: Say "m" sound instead of "n" with Ghunnah for 2 beats.

**Example Words**: مِنْ بَعْدِ, أَنْبَاء, سَمِيعٌ بَصِير

---

### 7. 🔵 **Idghaam with Ghunnah** - Blue
**Color**: `#2196F3` (Material Blue)

**Rule**: A specific type of Idghaam where the merging includes nasal sound (Ghunnah).

**Letters**: ي ن م و (from Yarmaloon)

**Duration**: 2 beats of Ghunnah

**Example Words**: مَنْ يَقُول, مِنْ مَاء

---

### 8. ⚪ **Izhar (Clear Pronunciation)** - No Color
**Rule**: Clear pronunciation of ن (Noon) Sakinah or Tanween before throat letters:
- ء (Hamza)
- ه (Ha)
- ع (Ayn)
- ح (Ha)
- غ (Ghayn)
- خ (Kha)

**Example Words**: مِنْ أَنْفُسِهِمْ, مِنْ عِلْم

---

### 9. 🔵 **Hamzat Wasl** - Cyan/Light Blue
**Color**: `#00BCD4` (Material Cyan)

**Rule**: A Hamza (ء) that is pronounced when starting but dropped when continuing from the previous word.

**Example Words**: اِقْرَأْ, اِعْبُدُوا, اَلْحَمْدُ

---

### 10. ⚪ **Silent Letters** - Light Gray
**Color**: `#BDBDBD` (Material Light Gray)

**Rule**: Letters that are written but not pronounced in certain contexts.

**Example**: ا in وَيَمْحُوا۟ (the small circle above indicates silence)

## 🛑 Pause Marks (Waqf/وَقْف)

### Mandatory and Recommended Stops

| Symbol | Name | Meaning | Color | Action |
|--------|------|---------|-------|--------|
| **م** or **ۘ** | Waqf Lazim | **MUST** stop | 🔴 Dark Red | Mandatory stop |
| **ق** or **ۗ** | Qala | **Better** to stop | 🟠 Orange | Preferred stop |
| **ج** or **ۖ** | Ja'iz | **May** stop | 🟢 Green | Permissible |
| **ص** or **ۚ** | Sila | **Better** to continue | 🔵 Blue | Preferred continuation |
| **ل** | La Waqf | Do **NOT** stop | 🔴 Red | Must continue |
| **ص ل** | Either/Or | Stop at one, not both | 🟡 Amber | Choose one |

## 🎯 How to Use the Color System

### For Students:
1. **Read Normally**: First, read the verse focusing on meaning
2. **Observe Colors**: Notice which words have Tajweed colors
3. **Apply Rules**: Practice applying the Tajweed rule for each colored word
4. **Listen & Compare**: Play the audio recitation and compare
5. **Record Yourself**: Use assignment mode to record and self-assess

### For Teachers:
1. **Point Out Colors**: Draw students' attention to colored words
2. **Explain Rules**: Use this guide to explain each color's meaning
3. **Practice Drills**: Focus on one rule at a time
4. **Audio Examples**: Play expert recitations highlighting specific rules
5. **Assess Progress**: Use assignment submissions to track improvement

## 🎓 Tajweed Levels

### Level 1: Beginner
- Recognize the six throat letters (Izhar)
- Learn basic Madd (2 beats)
- Identify Qalqalah letters

### Level 2: Intermediate
- Apply Ghunnah correctly
- Distinguish Idghaam types
- Practice Ikhfa with 15 letters

### Level 3: Advanced
- Master all Madd durations
- Perfect Iqlab pronunciation
- Understand pause mark variations
- Smooth, fluent recitation
