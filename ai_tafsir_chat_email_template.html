Example: Tafsir of Surah Al-Infitar (82), Verse 1

إِذَا السَّمَاءُ انفَطَرَتْ
"When the sky breaks apart"
NuraAI Explains (Based on Tafsir Ibn Kathir):

Imam Ibn Kathir explains that the word 'انفَطَرَتْ' (infatarat) used in this verse means 'to split asunder' or 'cleave apart.' This verse vividly describes one of the profound and awe-inspiring events that will occur on the Day of Resurrection.

On that momentous Day, the sky, which we see as a vast and stable expanse, will lose its current form and tear apart. This is a consistent theme in the Quran, where Allah describes the dramatic cosmic changes preceding the Final Judgment. For example, similar descriptions can be found in:

  • Surah Al-Muzzammil (73:18): 'The heaven will be rent asunder thereby?'
  • Surah Al-Haaqqah (69:16): 'And the heaven will be rent asunder, for that Day it (heaven) will be frail and weak.'

The splitting of the sky signifies the end of the current world order and the commencement of ultimate accountability before Allah. This event serves as a powerful reminder of Allah's omnipotence and the absolute certainty of the Last Day, urging humanity to reflect and prepare for it through faith and righteous actions.
