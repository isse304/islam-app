Surah An-Naba, Verse 1

عَمَّ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ
Translation: What are they asking one another about?

Can you explain the context behind this verse and what is being addressed in the subsequent verses of Surah An-Naba?

Tafsir Insights: Surah An-Naba, Verse 1

In Surah An-Naba (78), verse 1, Allah addresses the idolaters who deny the occurrence of the Day of Judgment. The verse emphasizes that they are questioning about a significant event, "the great news," which is the Day of Judgment. It highlights how people are divided into believers and disbelievers regarding this crucial matter.

Allah warns those who reject the concept of the Day of Judgment by stating emphatically that they will come to know its reality eventually. This serves as a severe threat and a direct warning to those who deny this fundamental aspect of faith.

Further Context from Surah An-Naba:

The subsequent verses in Surah An-Naba further elaborate on Allah's power and ability to create extraordinary things as evidence of His capability to resurrect the dead. These include:

  • The creation of Earth as a stable resting place.
  • Mountains as pegs for stability.
  • Human beings in pairs for procreation.
  • Sleep for rest, and day and night for livelihood.
  • The heavens with their perfection.
  • Rain from clouds for sustenance and growth.

All these serve as signs pointing towards Allah's greatness and His ability to bring about resurrection on the Day of Judgment.
